StepWise

Visual, step-by-step lessons for autistic learners

A mobile learning app that teaches English and Maths to autistic learners aged 10–15 in small, visual, step-by-step lessons. One idea per screen, a predictable routine, a listen button on every instruction, calm rewards, a break that is always one tap away, and a parent/teacher dashboard.
