Product / UX design

UX case studies

Projects from my Google UX Design Professional Certificate, designed in Figma. Each follows research, wireframes and prototyping.

UX case study · 01

Calmly

Problem. Assistive apps tend to come one disability at a time, priced in dollars, tuned for fast data and Western defaults. One calm companion for blind, low-vision and autistic users, built for Nigeria.

  1. Research Desk research with cited sources, and two proto-personas
  2. Wireframes Four key user flows: count money, calm button, routine, caregiver setup
  3. Prototype 10 hi-fi screens, design system, WCAG 2.2 AA
  4. Usability testing Planned: TalkBack sessions with blind and low-vision adults

UX case study · 02

StepWise

Problem. Most learning apps are designed to excite: bright palettes, confetti, autoplaying sound, dense text and a different layout on every screen. Autistic learners aged 10–15 need the opposite.

  1. Research Secondary research on autism in Nigeria (WHO, UNICEF, W3C, TEACCH) and proto-personas
  2. Wireframes Greyscale lo-fi versions of five key screens
  3. Prototype 30 hi-fi iOS screens, design system, WCAG 2.2 checklist
  4. Usability testing Planned, not yet run
